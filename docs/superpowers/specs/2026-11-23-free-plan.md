# Gistsmith permanent free plan

Status: design spec for the owner's decision. Docs only, no product code. Date: 2026-11-23.

Goal: a free plan that never loses money, answers Granola's free tier and turns free users into Personal or Pro
subscribers without taking sales away from Personal.

Today there is only a trial: `TRIAL_MEETINGS=3` or `TRIAL_DAYS=7` from the first trial meeting, whichever comes first,
at most `TRIAL_MAX_MEETING_MINUTES=90` per meeting (`server/wrangler.jsonc`). When it ends, every server feature stops
(`402 trial_exhausted`, `subscription_required`).

Paid plans (`wrangler.jsonc`, `Packages/AINotepadCore/Sources/AINotepadCore/Hosted/HostedPlans.swift`; prices include
taxes):

| Plan | Price | Meeting hours | Longest meeting | AI tokens per month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | $5.99/mo or $49/yr | `MONTHLY_CAP_HOURS=10` | 240 min | `MONTHLY_TOKEN_CAP=6.5 M` |
| Pro (off in production, `PRO_TIER_POLICY=off`) | $12.99/mo or $109/yr | 30 | 240 min | 19.5 M |

Every fact below comes from the code or config at `e178e11a`. Assumptions are labelled **Assumption**.

## 1. Cost model from the code

### 1.1 Unit prices the repo states

| Item | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Final transcription, ElevenLabs Scribe | $0.22 per audio hour | `SCRIBE_USD_PER_HOUR` |
| Keyterms surcharge (names, vocabulary) | +$0.05 per hour when the request has keyterms | `SCRIBE_KEYTERMS_USD_PER_HOUR`, `server/src/transcribe.ts:509` |
| Live transcript, Deepgram Nova-3 | $0.0092 per minute per stream ($0.552 per hour) | `DEEPGRAM_USD_PER_MINUTE` |
| Claude Haiku 5.5, prompt ≤ 100 K tokens | $0.10 / M input, $0.50 / M output | `server/src/usage.ts:12-13` |
| Claude Haiku 5.5, prompt > 100 K tokens | $0.50 / M input, $2.50 / M output | `server/src/usage.ts:14-16` |
| Prompt cache | write 1.25 × input, read 0.1 × input | `server/src/usage.ts:44-45` |
| Web search in Ask | $10 per 1,000 searches; at most 3 per answer and `MONTHLY_WEB_SEARCH_CAP=100` per user per month (≤ $1.00/month) | `wrangler.jsonc` comment above `MONTHLY_WEB_SEARCH_CAP` |

The server uses one model for every call (`hostedModel`, `server/src/llm/request.ts:7`). It refuses a call whose input
is estimated above `LLM_MAX_INPUT_TOKENS=100000` (UTF-8 bytes / 3, `server/src/llm/proxy.ts:416-418`). So the cheap
short-prompt price applies to every call in practice.

The admin's unit economics (`server/src/adminProduct.ts:414-477`) use the same rates: LLM tokens × the short-prompt
price, Scribe seconds × `SCRIBE_USD_PER_HOUR`, Deepgram seconds × `DEEPGRAM_USD_PER_MINUTE`.

### 1.2 How a hosted meeting is processed today

1. **Live transcript.** Mac records two tracks: "Sen" (microphone) and "Diğerleri" (system audio). iPhone and Mac
   in-person mode record "Diğerleri" only.
   - "Sen" on Mac always streams to Deepgram while `DEEPGRAM_POLICY=on`. Its live text **is** the saved transcript; the
     final pass does not redo it (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-on-device-live-preview.md`).
   - "Diğerleri" is written on the device with Apple's `SpeechAnalyzer` for users in the on-device rollout
     (`ON_DEVICE_LIVE_PERCENT=25` in production, `server/src/onDevice.ts`; route in
     `Hosted/HostedLiveRoute.swift`). Everyone else streams it to Deepgram too.
2. **Final pass.** The hosted build always re-transcribes "Diğerleri" on the server with Scribe
   (`App/AppModel.swift:2709-2712`, `App/Hosted/HostedSeams.swift:242` `hostedFinalPassPlan`; iPhone
   `iOS/PhoneRecorder.swift:1274`).
3. **Note.** A transcript of up to 20,000 characters gets one `note` call. A longer one gets one `note_chunk` call per
   20,000 characters plus one `note_overview` call (`Meeting/ChunkedNote.swift:8`, `:391-403`). Speech runs at about
   1,750 characters a minute (comment at `ChunkedNote.swift:5-7`).
4. **Optional extras**, not part of "one AI note":
   - live translation, measured at 0.66 M tokens per hour (`wrangler.jsonc`, budget comment);
   - transcript translation when the note language differs from the meeting language: 2,400 characters per request,
     a ~700-token system prompt, ~600 source tokens and ~1.2 K output tokens (`Meeting/TranscriptTranslation.swift:29-35`);
   - speaker review, which the user starts with a button (`App/AppModel.swift:5129`, `iOS/AINotepadiOSApp.swift:2318`).

### 1.4 Token size of one AI note

Planning rate: 13 K tokens per note call. It comes from the measured 4-hour budget in `wrangler.jsonc`: "21 parts ×
~13 K + merge ≈ 300 K". **Assumption:** the overview call costs the same 13 K as a chunk.

| Meeting | Transcript | Calls | Tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 min | 30 × 1,750 = 52,500 chars | ⌈52,500 / 20,000⌉ = 3 chunks + 1 overview = 4 | 4 × 13 K = **52 K** |
| 60 min | 105,000 chars | ⌈105,000 / 20,000⌉ = 6 chunks + 1 overview = 7 | 7 × 13 K = **91 K** |

Cross-check: the same method gives 117 K for 90 minutes, and `wrangler.jsonc` states a measured ~100 K. The planning
figure is about 17% on the high side, which is the safe direction.

**Assumption:** 20% of note tokens are output. A chunk returns short JSON (summary, decisions, tasks) for 20,000
characters of input. The blended price is then 0.8 × $0.10 + 0.2 × $0.50 = **$0.18 per M tokens**.
The hard upper bound, every token at the output price, is $0.50 per M.

| Note | Expected (× $0.18/M) | Upper bound (× $0.50/M) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min, 52 K tokens | $0.0094 (0.9 ¢) | $0.026 |
| 60 min, 91 K tokens | $0.0164 (1.6 ¢) | $0.046 |

### 1.5 One AI note: (a) cloud final transcription vs (b) on-device only

**(a) Cloud final transcription (today's hosted path).**

Scribe with keyterms costs $0.27 per hour: 30 min = $0.135, 60 min = $0.27. The live stream depends on the device:

| Device and route | Live (Deepgram) | Final (Scribe) | Note | **Total 30 min** | Live | Final | Note | **Total 60 min** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone, or Mac in-person, on-device preview | $0 | $0.135 | $0.009 | **$0.144** | $0 | $0.27 | $0.016 | **$0.286** |
| Mac online meeting, in the rollout (1 stream, "Sen") | 30 × 0.0092 = $0.276 | $0.135 | $0.009 | **$0.420** | $0.552 | $0.27 | $0.016 | **$0.838** |
| Mac online meeting, outside the rollout (2 streams; 75% of production users) | $0.552 | $0.135 | $0.009 | **$0.696** | $1.104 | $0.27 | $0.016 | **$1.390** |

Optional extras on top: live translation ≈ 0.66 M tokens × $0.18/M ≈ $0.12 per hour. Transcript translation for a
30-minute meeting is ⌈52,500 / 2,400⌉ = 22 requests. **Assumption:** ~2.1 K input tokens (prompt, source, context) and
1.2 K output tokens each. That is 46 K × $0.10/M + 26 K × $0.50/M ≈ $0.018, and about $0.036 for 60 minutes.

**(b) On-device transcription only.** Live and final transcript on the device (Apple speech): $0 audio.

| | 30 min | 60 min |
|---|---|---|
| Transcription | $0 | $0 |
| AI note, expected | **$0.0094** | **$0.0164** |
| AI note, upper bound | $0.026 | $0.046 |

An on-device note costs about **1/15 of the iPhone cloud path and 1/45 to 1/85 of the Mac cloud path.** Audio is
94-99% of a meeting's cost. A free plan is only cheap if the server refuses every audio cost for it.

### 1.6 Ask

`wrangler.jsonc` states one Ask answer at ~10-30 K tokens, with `LLM_ASK_MAX_TOKENS=8192` output (≤ $0.004 of output).
Planning figure: 20 K tokens per question, $0.0036 expected and ≤ $0.010 upper bound. Web search costs $0.01 per search
plus result tokens, so it is excluded from the free plan.

## 2. Plan proposal

### 2.1 Recommended: Free = on-device transcripts + 4 AI notes a month (after the trial)

| | Free (recommended) | Personal |
|---|---|---|
| Recording and transcript | Unlimited length and count, **on this device** (Apple speech; WhisperKit on macOS 15-25), no speaker labels, no name hints | 10 h/month of cloud transcript with speakers and names, up to 240 min; **after the 10 h, recording continues on-device** (new, so Personal is never below Free) |
| AI notes | **4 per calendar month (UTC)**, meetings up to **45 min** | Every meeting within 10 h |
| Ask (incl. keyboard Ask) | **5 questions per month**, from this device's meetings | ~50-100 per month (`MONTHLY_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_CAP`) |
| Web search in Ask | No | Up to 100 per month |
| Live translation, "Ne kaçırdım?", live questions, transcript translation | No | Yes |
| Mac ↔ iPhone sync, AI assistant connector (MCP) | No | Yes |
| Task integrations (Linear, Jira, Notion: `task_agent`) | No | Yes |
| Note history | Unlimited, on the device | Unlimited, synced |

**Why this shape.**

- **Audio is the whole cost** (§1.5). With on-device transcription only, a free note costs ~1-2 ¢, and the server can
  refuse every audio cost outright.
- **Transcripts are unlimited because they cost us nothing.** Notes are the metered "aha". The free user records every
  meeting and still sees each limit at a real moment of need:
  - the 5th note of the month;
  - a meeting over 45 minutes;
  - the 6th question;
  - a missing speaker label;
  - wanting the meeting on the other device.
  Each is an honest upgrade prompt (spec 2026-11-08 F3: "Prompt people to subscribe at relevant times").
- **4 notes a month of at most 45 minutes** is one per week: enough to form the habit (a weekly 1:1 or stand-up), too
  few for anyone with real meeting load. That is at most 3 hours of notes, against Personal's 10 hours (about 10-20
  notes). A 60-minute cap was rejected in review: 4 × 60 min would cover the reference payer of §2.4 entirely.
- **Personal keeps the clear quality gap:** cloud transcript with speakers and name hints, live features, sync, Ask at
  volume, longer meetings, integrations. Free does not offer a cheaper copy of any Personal feature. It offers the
  on-device version that Personal replaces.
- **Note history: no limit.** Granola limits free history to 30 days, but Granola stores notes on its servers. Our free
  notes live only on the user's device (free users get no sync, see below). Hiding or deleting the user's own local
  files would save us nothing and would read as hostile. The gates are sync and Ask volume instead.
- **No sync for free.** `syncR2StoredBytesBudget` (8 GB) and the R2 operation budgets (`server/src/sync.ts:52-56`) are
  global. Free accounts could use up storage that payers need. "Your notes on Mac and iPhone" stays a paid reason.

**When the free plan is open.** After the trial, as a floor:

- the trial ends (meetings used or 7 days) without a purchase;
- a subscription ends;
- an account's trial ended before launch (one-time win-back).

New accounts still start with the full cloud trial (§5).

### 2.2 Server limits (all configuration, tunable without a release)

| Var | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `FREE_PLAN_POLICY` | `off` (prod), `on` (dev/tests) | Kill switch; see §3.3 for `FREE_PLAN_USERS` |
| `FREE_NOTES_PER_MONTH` | 4 | Note grants per UTC month |
| `FREE_MAX_MEETING_MINUTES` | 45 | Longest meeting a free note covers (the client checks the transcript length; the server enforces the token budget) |
| `FREE_NOTE_TOKEN_BUDGET` | 90,000 | Tokens per note grant. A 45-min note is 78,750 chars → 4 chunks + overview = 65 K, plus ~40% for retries |
| `FREE_LLM_MAX_INPUT_TOKENS` | 30,000 | Estimated input per free call (instead of `LLM_MAX_INPUT_TOKENS=100000`). A note chunk is ~10 K. 30 K estimated means ≤ 90 KB of text, which can't tokenise past 100 K, so the long-prompt price can't apply. Free calls carry no images |
| (client) free Ask context | — | Ask already cuts its archive to `archiveChars` 60,000 characters (`Meeting/NoteLimits.swift:21`), about 22 K estimated tokens, so it fits the 30 K limit with its prompt. For free accounts the app keeps that cut and never sends more; if the server still answers `payload_too_large`, Ask retries once with half the context and then shows a clear message, never a raw error |
| `FREE_ASKS_PER_MONTH` | 5 | Settled `ask_answer` + `keyboard_ask` answers per UTC month; `ask_search_terms` does not count |
| `FREE_MONTHLY_ACCOUNT_TOKEN_CAP` | 150,000 | Backstop over all free calls outside a note grant (Ask, note edit, follow-up email). With the 30 K input limit, one question can't drain the month |
| `FREE_ACCOUNTS_PER_IP_PER_DAY` | 3 | Accounts per IP per day that get their first free note grant (the trial's version is 10 in production, 3 in dev) |
| `DAILY_FREE_SPEND_CEILING_USD` | 1 | All free spend per UTC day: paces spend so the month can't be used up in a few days; `503 free_capacity_reached` |
| `MONTHLY_FREE_SPEND_CEILING_USD` | 30 | All free spend per UTC month: the hard cost cap; alert at 80% |

### 2.3 Worst case per free user per month

| Case | Tokens | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Audio, Deepgram, web search | 0 (server refuses) | **$0** |
| Strict bound: every budget fully used, every token at the output price, plus one overrunning call per budget | 4 × 90 K + 150 K = 510 K | 510 K × $0.50/M = $0.255, + 5 × $0.006 overrun = **$0.29** |
| Realistic ceiling: every budget fully used, 20% output | 510 K | 510 K × $0.18/M = **$0.092** |
| Typical active user: 4 × 30-min notes + 5 questions at 20 K | 4 × 52 K + 100 K = 308 K | **$0.055** |
| Inactive free account | 0 | $0 |

Why the strict bound holds:

- **The short-prompt price always applies.** Free calls are refused above 30 K estimated input (≤ 90 KB of text,
  `FREE_LLM_MAX_INPUT_TOKENS`), which stays under the 100 K threshold even if crafted text tokenises at one token per
  byte. Free calls carry no images.
- **Overrun is small and counted.** The reservation system books estimate + `max_tokens` before a call and the real
  usage after it (`server/src/reservations.ts:405-436`). So the last call on a budget can overrun it by at most its real
  input minus its estimate: ≤ 60 K tokens × $0.10/M = $0.006, once per budget (4 grants + 1 account budget).
- **No audio route.** Free accounts never get a meeting session, so transcription and Deepgram are refused.
- **Global cap.** `MONTHLY_FREE_SPEND_CEILING_USD` bounds the total whatever happens per account.

### 2.4 Break-even conversion rate against Personal

**Net revenue per Personal payer per month.** Apple takes 15% (`APP_STORE_COMMISSION_RATE=0.15`); Stripe takes about 3%
(owner's figure). VAT is ignored here. The prices include taxes (`HostedPlans.swift:3-4`), so see the VAT row.

| Channel | Monthly plan | Yearly plan (÷ 12) |
|---|---|---|
| App Store | 5.99 × 0.85 = **$5.09** | 49 × 0.85 / 12 = **$3.47** |
| Stripe | 5.99 × 0.97 = **$5.81** | 49 × 0.97 / 12 = **$3.96** |
| Same, if 25.5% VAT is inside the price (Finland rate from `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-28-abonelik-surumu.md:32`) | $4.06 / $4.63 | $2.77 / $3.16 |

**Payer cost.** **Assumption:** a reference payer records 4 hours a month (Personal allows 10). From §1.5, an iPhone
payer costs 4 × $0.286 = $1.14 and a Mac payer in the on-device rollout costs 4 × $0.838 = $3.35.

Margin M per payer per month:

- App Store yearly, iPhone: 3.47 − 1.14 = **$2.33**
- Stripe monthly, Mac: 5.81 − 3.35 = **$2.46**
- Stripe yearly, Mac: 3.96 − 3.35 = **$0.61** (thin, because of Deepgram)
- Any Mac payer outside the on-device rollout (75% of production Mac users) at 4 h: 4 × $1.39 = $5.56 of cost,
  more than any Personal plan's net revenue. That is a loss before the free plan exists, hence R5.

**Break-even.** A free user costs F per month and a converted user earns M per month. The free plan pays for itself
when the share p of active free users who convert satisfies p × M ≥ (1 − p) × F, that is p ≥ F / (M + F).

| F (per active free user per month) | M = $2.33 (reference) | M = $0.61 (Mac yearly) |
|---|---|---|
| $0.055 (typical) | **2.3%** | 8.3% |
| $0.092 (realistic ceiling) | **3.8%** | 13.1% |
| $0.29 (strict bound) | 11.1% | 32.2% |

Two caveats on M:

- The Mac Deepgram cost is paid today from a one-time `DEEPGRAM_BUDGET_USD=185` credit. When the credit runs out, new
  meetings fall back to WhisperKit (`wrangler.jsonc`, comment above `DEEPGRAM_POLICY`). So $0.61 is the list-price
  margin, not today's cash margin.
- The repo states no Stripe fee (R6).

For comparison, RevenueCat 2026 reports a 2.1% D35 freemium median and 10.7% for a hard paywall (finding F9 in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-subscription-conversion.md`). That is install-to-paid by day 35, a different
denominator from p (paying share of active free users), so it is a rough reference only. What this means:

- At typical use the free plan roughly breaks even on its own conversions. It is acquisition spend, capped by
  `MONTHLY_FREE_SPEND_CEILING_USD`, not a profit centre.
- It only stays profitable if it does not lower the trial's own conversion (§6, R1).
- Payer margin is the bigger lever. Mac yearly payers on two Deepgram streams make the break-even 4× worse. Raising
  `ON_DEVICE_LIVE_PERCENT` to 100 (already planned in the on-device preview spec) matters more than any free-plan number.
- Inactive free accounts cost $0, so measured against all free accounts the rate is lower.

### 2.5 Options considered and not recommended

| Option | Cost per free user per month | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| **B.** Free with cloud transcription, 2 notes/month | iPhone 2 × $0.286 = $0.57; Mac up to 2 × $1.39 = $2.78 | 5-50× the recommended cost; the break-even exceeds any freemium benchmark; it hands out the Personal product |
| **C.** Fully on-device, notes too (Apple Intelligence) | $0 | 4,096-token context (`OnDeviceLLM.swift:7`), no Finnish, Apple Intelligence devices only. The free note would be our weakest note, and the note is what converts. Keep it as a fallback if free spend runs over |

## 3. Abuse limits

### 3.1 Accounts

Accounts exist only through Sign in with Apple or Google; the server has no other way to create one
(`meetings.ts`, comment above `startTrialMeeting`). Free usage follows the identity, not the account:

- extend `trial_identity_ledger` (`server/src/trialLedger.ts`), which already carries the month's usage across account
  deletion, with `free_notes_month`, `free_notes_used` and `free_asks_used`;
- deleting and re-creating the account in the same month then resets nothing.

### 3.2 Device and IP

- **DeviceCheck `bit1`** is unused today (`server/src/deviceCheck.ts:14`; `bit0` = "gave a trial"). It is Apple's last
  free bit for this app, so this uses it up for good. Proposal:
  `bit1` = "this device has a free plan bound to an account". The first free note grant on a device binds it, the same
  way `requireTrialDevice` binds `bit0` (`server/src/meetings.ts:330-376`). Another account on that device gets
  `403 free_device_used` ("Subscribe to use Gistsmith on this device with another account").
  - Production already has `DEVICECHECK_POLICY=required`.
  - Mac and iPhone both send DeviceCheck tokens through `App/Hosted/HostedAccount.swift:1626-1650` (shared by iOS).
- **IP:** `FREE_ACCOUNTS_PER_IP_PER_DAY` uses the trial's `clientIpHash` primitive (`server/src/app.ts:458`), counted at
  the account's first free note grant.
- **Existing rate limiters apply unchanged:** `START_USER_LIMITER` (note grants), `PROXY_USER_LIMITER` (LLM calls),
  `AUTH_IP_LIMITER`, `API_IP_LIMITER`.
- **App Attest** stays `optional`; it is reported on the grant for logs, as on meeting starts.
- **A grant is a capped Haiku proxy.** The client supplies the prompt, and the 45-minute and 2-minute checks run only
  in the client. Someone could spend a grant on anything other than a note. That is accepted: the spend stays inside
  the grant's budget and the purpose allowlist.

### 3.3 Global switches

- `FREE_PLAN_POLICY`: `off` means no account is free: today's behaviour, trial then paywall. `on` means the free plan
  is open. `FREE_PLAN_USERS` is an allowlist of user IDs that get the free plan while the policy is `off`, for the owner
  and testers. Same pattern as `WATCH_RECORDING_POLICY` / `WATCH_RECORDING_USERS`.
- `DAILY_FREE_SPEND_CEILING_USD` and `MONTHLY_FREE_SPEND_CEILING_USD`. Free spend is counted apart from trial and paid
  spend, like `DAILY_TRIAL_SPEND_CEILING_USD`:
  - `daily_spend.free_micro_usd` and `usage_reservations.free_spend`;
  - the check copies `requireTrialSpendHeadroom` (`server/src/usage.ts:265`);
  - free spend is also part of the global `DAILY_SPEND_CEILING_USD` ($50 in production). Free use can take at most
    the free daily ceiling ($1) of it, so free accounts can't use up what subscribers need. The trial ceiling ($25)
    works the same way.
- A refusal never loses data. The transcript is already on the device. The note waits with "Free notes are paused
  today; your transcript is saved" and retries on the next launch or day. Alerts `daily_free_spend_ceiling_reached` and
  `monthly_free_spend_ceiling_reached` go to the ops log.

## 5. Rollout

1. **Server, dark.** Ships with `FREE_PLAN_POLICY=off` in production; dev and tests `on`. Migration 0069 is additive.
   Old apps see nothing new, because free fields are only sent when the policy or allowlist applies.
2. **Version gate.** The server reports `plan: "free"` only to apps whose `x-app-version` is at or above the first
   free-aware build (a new `FREE_PLAN_MIN_BUILD` var). Older apps keep today's "trial ended" state, so they never try a
   cloud meeting that the server would refuse.
3. **Apps.** Mac and iPhone release with the free-aware build, and the owner and testers are on `FREE_PLAN_USERS`. The
   owner checks a full month on one device: 4 notes, the 5th refused, Ask limit, too-long meeting, ceiling refusal
   (forced with a tiny ceiling in dev).
4. **Site, terms, privacy** merge together with turning `FREE_PLAN_POLICY=on` (one `wrangler.jsonc` PR). Ceilings start
   at $1/day and $30/month.
5. **Measure for 4-6 weeks** in admin:
   - trial → paid (baseline from the 4 weeks before);
   - free → paid;
   - subscriber churn to Free (cancellations followed by free note use);
   - free spend per active free user;
   - OS and language coverage (`free_unavailable_*`).

   Rollback is setting the policy to `off`: free accounts return to "trial ended", nothing is deleted, and local notes
   stay on the device.

**Interaction with the trial: trial, then free (recommended).** The trial gives the best first impression (cloud
transcript with speakers, live features). Free keeps non-buyers in the product, so they meet the later upgrade moments
instead of churning at the paywall. Accounts whose trial ended before launch become free when the policy turns on. That
is a one-time win-back with a "You're on Free" notice.

## 6. Risks and open questions for the owner

| # | Risk or question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Cannibalisation of the trial.** Hard paywalls convert ~5× better than freemium (10.7% vs 2.1%). Users may skip buying at trial end. | Trial then free, as recommended. At trial end the paywall stays the main screen, and "Continue on Free (4 notes a month)" is the secondary action (not hidden, not shamed). Guard metric: if trial → paid drops more than a third against the pre-launch baseline for 4 weeks, turn the policy off |
| R2 | Free transcript quality is lower. The 21 dictation-only languages (Turkish and Finnish included) come without punctuation, and there are no speaker labels and no name hints. Gistsmith may look worse. | Accept. It is labelled honestly ("Written on this device; Personal adds speakers and names") and the trial shows the real quality first |
| R1b | **Subscriber churn to Free.** "Subscription ends → Free" makes downgrading painless for light payers. | Accept, with a guard: Free lacks speakers, sync, live features, Ask volume and meetings over 45 min, and Personal now keeps on-device recording past 10 h, so Personal is never below Free. Watch "churn to Free" monthly. If it exceeds new free → paid, cut Free to 3 notes |
| R3 | The Apple speech path needs macOS 26 / iOS 26; the apps support macOS 15 / iOS 18. | Mac: macOS 15-25 uses the hosted WhisperKit engine that already ships (live text saved as the transcript). iPhone: iOS 26 only, with an explanation. | R4 | Apple has no on-device recognition for 5 meeting languages (et, bg, lv, lt, fa). | Mac: WhisperKit covers them. iPhone: Free is unavailable for meetings in those languages (trial then paywall, with an honest line) |
| R5 | Payer margin is thin for Mac yearly payers on two Deepgram streams ($0.61), which makes the break-even 4× worse. | Raise `ON_DEVICE_LIVE_PERCENT` to 100 before or with the free launch, as the on-device preview spec already plans after its device check |

| R7 | Ceiling values. A paused free plan is a bad look for an advertised plan. | Start at $1/day (paces spend) and $30/month (hard cap, alert at 80%). That pays for ~545 typical active free users a month, or ~100 at the strict bound. Raise it when free → paid margin covers it; review monthly |

## 7. Review

One independent red-team reviewer (2026-11-23) read the draft against the code. The reviewer confirmed the arithmetic
and most references, and raised 1 critical, 6 important and 6 minor findings.

**Adopted:**

- **C1, Free beat Personal.** Unlimited on-device recording beat Personal's 10 h cap, and 4 × 60-min notes covered the
  reference payer. Free notes are now capped at 45 min, Personal keeps on-device recording past 10 h, and churn to Free
  is tracked (R1b).
- **I1, the strict bound wasn't hard.** The 100 K input check is an estimate (bytes / 3), and the last call can overrun
  a budget. Added `FREE_LLM_MAX_INPUT_TOKENS=30000` and no images on free calls; the overrun is in the bound.
- **I2, grant schema.** Added `tokens_used`, `first_settled_at`, `refunded_at` and `usage_reservations.free_note_id`,
  plus a grant step in `settleLLMStatements`.
- **I3, entry paths.** Covered imports, call recordings, Watch and `keyboardAsk.ts`.
- **I4, the 26+ gate on Mac was wrong.** The hosted Mac already ships WhisperKit, so Free runs on macOS 15+. Only
  iPhone needs 26.
- **I5, ceilings.** $1/day paces spend under the $30/month cap.
- **I6, 5 questions couldn't be guaranteed.** Account calls now have a 30 K input limit and a 150 K backstop, and only
  answers count.
- **M1-M6.**
  - Deepgram one-time credit and the Stripe fee caveat.
  - Benchmark denominator.
  - Five PhoneRecorder call sites.
  - Production trial IP limit.
  - The global-ceiling sentence.
  - Conversion triggers, `ServerParityTests`, `hostedAskUnavailableReason`, win-back targeting, Watch copy, the Debug
    flag.
  - `bit1` is the last DeviceCheck bit.
  - A grant is a capped proxy.

**Not adopted:** cutting Free to 3 notes now. One note a week is the habit we want to build, and the 45-min cap closes
the overlap with Personal. The 3-note step is kept as the R1b response if churn to Free shows up.
