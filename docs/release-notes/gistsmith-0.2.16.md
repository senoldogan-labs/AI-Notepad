Gistsmith 0.2.16 makes moving between meetings and typing in Ask quick again, and makes the Gistsmith Keyboard more responsive.

## Improvements
- **Quicker meetings.** Opening a meeting, going back to the list and switching between long meetings no longer stalls, even with hundreds of meetings and hours-long transcripts.
- **Smooth typing in Ask.** A long question types without lag, and the answers above the field stay put while you write.
- **A more responsive keyboard (iPhone).** Taps register at once, including fast typing on languages with more keys per row, such as Turkish, and while you type a question to Gistsmith.
- **The voice field always shows (iPhone).** The moving field in the dictation panel no longer goes blank while you speak.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
