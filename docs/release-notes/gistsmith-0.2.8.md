Gistsmith 0.2.8 lets you choose the app icon and fixes a set of smaller problems found in a full review before launch.

## New
- **Choose your app icon.** Pick an icon that matches your look in Appearance settings, on Mac and iPhone.

## Improvements
- **Sleep never counts as meeting time (Mac).** If your Mac goes to sleep during a recording, the recording ends and the sleep is not counted toward your monthly hours.
- **Audio stays where it was recorded.** Automatic audio clean-up on one device no longer marks a meeting recorded on your other device as "audio deleted".
- **Better English on-device transcription.** Outside English-speaking countries, on-device speech now uses US English instead of a less common regional variant.
- **Crash reports send in one go.** "Send" on the crash notice now sends every new report at once.
- **Speaker names everywhere (Mac).** The meeting page in the recording panel shows speaker names, like the main window.
- **Exports in another language are complete.** Exporting or sharing a note in another language waits for an up-to-date translation, so newly added tasks are never missing.
- **Export works without a note (iPhone).** Markdown and Word export of the transcript work even while a note language is selected.
- **Your calendar choice stays (iPhone).** Opening setup again no longer turns the calendar back on.
- **Task sends finish (Mac and iPhone).** Sending many tasks to Jira or Linear no longer stops halfway with a "reconnect" message.
- **The sample meeting stays a sample.** Its made-up people no longer appear in People, and connecting Apple Notes or a folder no longer writes it there.
- **Safer sign-out and account switching.** Signing out while the app refreshes your sign-in, or switching accounts while syncing, never mixes the two accounts.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
