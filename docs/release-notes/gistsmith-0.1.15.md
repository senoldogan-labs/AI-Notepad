Gistsmith 0.1.15 can use what was on screen when it writes your meeting notes, and Settings are easier to read.

## New
- **Screens in your notes.** Turn on "Use screen snapshots in notes" in Settings › General, and when a meeting's note is written Gistsmith includes up to six of the meeting's key screen snapshots, so the note can point to what was shown, like "on the Pricing slide, 12:40". On the meeting's Screens tab, untick any snapshot you don't want used. It is off until you turn it on; with it off, snapshots never leave your Mac.

## Improvements
- **Calmer Settings.** Long explanations now sit behind a small question mark next to each setting; click it to read more.
- **Sharing in another language** now uses the meeting's current title after you rename it.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
