Gistsmith 0.2.10 gives the Mac a cleaner Ask page, lets longer note edits finish and keeps your questions on the meeting you just recorded.

## Improvements
- **A cleaner Ask page on Mac.** The empty Ask page now puts the greeting, the question box and your recent chats together in the middle of the window, in every look.
- **Longer notes finish.** Asking to make a note longer no longer stops halfway with a length error, even for long meetings.
- **Questions stay on the meeting you just recorded (Mac).** When a recording ends and its page opens, questions go to that meeting, even if the Ask panel was open before.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
