Gistsmith 0.1.20 brings reminders so nothing slips, a new People view and a tidier meeting summary.

## New
- **Reminders that keep you on track.** Gistsmith now tells you when a meeting's notes are ready, reminds you of your tasks on the day they are due (mark them done right from the notification), sends a short Monday summary of your open tasks, and gives one gentle nudge if a week passes without a recording. Each one can be turned off in Settings › Notifications.
- **People as a table.** See everyone you meet with in one sortable table — title, number of meetings, talk time and when you last met. Click the eye to open a person's details.
- **Listen in the background.** Meeting playback keeps going when you lock your iPhone or close the window, with play, pause and skip from the lock screen, Control Center, AirPods and your Mac's media keys.

## Improvements
- **A tidier meeting summary.** Your tasks come first, and tasks, topics, decisions and marked moments sit in tabs. Topics and task groups open and close, and finished tasks move to the bottom.
- **Due dates like "Friday" or "tomorrow"** are now understood, so those tasks get their reminder too.
- **Your language choices stick.** The language you pick for a meeting's notes and for your next recording is remembered.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
