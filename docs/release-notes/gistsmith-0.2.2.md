Gistsmith 0.2.2 brings a calmer meeting page that shows where every point came from, free personal insights, and voice typing that's easier to start with.

## New
- **See where a point came from.** Hover over any line of a note and click the magnifier to see the moments in the conversation it came from, with who said it and when. Click a quote to jump there and play it.
- **Ask about this meeting.** A bar at the bottom of every meeting answers questions about it, writes a follow-up email, lists the actions, gives a short summary or makes the note longer. The transcript opens from the same bar.
- **Insights, free for everyone.** A new page shows how many meetings you had, how much time they took, when you usually meet, who you meet most and how your tasks are going. Everything is worked out on your device.
- **A friendlier start to voice typing.** A short intro lets you test your shortcut and try a sentence. While you speak, a small pill shows a live sound wave, and you can turn on gentle start and stop sounds.

## Improvements
- **Connections, redesigned.** Every integration has its own page that explains what it does and how your data is used. AI assistant access starts folded, and it now tells you if any meetings weren't accepted, with a button to try again.
- **Auto-send from a locked iPhone.** Notes can now go to Slack, Notion, Linear and your webhooks while your iPhone stays locked in your pocket.
- **Every shortcut in one place.** Settings › Shortcuts lists every keyboard shortcut in the app.
- **Quieter settings.** Smaller switches and a simpler account picture in the sidebar.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
