Gistsmith 0.2.5 tells you exactly which AI services handle your meetings, lets you pick an app icon and tidies up Ask.

## New
- **You choose what goes to AI services.** Before anything leaves your Mac for the first time, a screen names each service, what it receives and why. Nothing is sent until you agree, and you can change your answer in Settings › Data and privacy.
- **Pick your app icon.** Choose from five icons in Settings › Appearance. On the Mac the Dock shows it while Gistsmith is open.

## Improvements
- **A calmer Ask.** Search results stay folded while the answer is written, rows are tighter, and the scope label no longer gets cut off.
- **Simpler themes.** Appearance now offers System, Light and Dark. If you used Paper, Sand, Midnight or Graphite, Gistsmith picks the closest one.
- **Colours that follow your accent.** The recording card and its band take on the accent colour you choose.
- **Steadier behind the scenes.** An update waits until a meeting has finished processing, and many smaller fixes.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
