Gistsmith 0.1.14 can keep what was shown on screen during a meeting, and voice typing feels instant.

## New
- **Shared-screen snapshots.** Turn on "Take snapshots of the shared screen" in Settings › General, and while you record an online meeting Gistsmith keeps a still image each time the shared screen changes: a new slide, a design frame, a document page. A new Screens tab in the meeting shows each image with the time it was on screen and what was said meanwhile; click the time to play the audio from there. Snapshots stay on your Mac, in the meeting's folder, and you can delete one or all of them. It is off until you turn it on, and macOS asks for Screen Recording permission the first time.
- **Screens in your AI tools.** Claude, Cursor and other apps connected through Settings › Connections can now list a meeting's screens and open each image together with the feedback given on it, so you can turn review comments into changes slide by slide.

## Improvements
- **Faster voice typing.** Your words now appear in the voice typing bar while you speak, and the text is typed as soon as you let go of the shortcut.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
