Gistsmith 0.2.13 lets you ask Gistsmith from the keyboard and get the answer right where you type, and gives voice typing new ways to shape your words.

## New
- **Ask Gistsmith without leaving your app (iPhone).** Ask a question about your meetings from the Gistsmith Keyboard and the answer appears in the keyboard: add it to your message or copy it, without opening Gistsmith. Needs Full Access, and answers come from your meetings when the AI assistant connection or Meeting notes in the keyboard is on.
- **Say the punctuation (iPhone).** Say "comma", "question mark" or "new line" while voice typing and the mark is written; say "first… second…" and your words become a list.

## Improvements
- **Cleaner voice typing (iPhone).** Ums and stutters are dropped, and you can choose whether a dictation ends with a period or is written all in lowercase.
- **Your keyboard, your way (iPhone).** Haptics in three strengths, the microphone key on the left or right of the space bar, and switches for auto-capitalization and key pop-ups.
- **A livelier voice field (iPhone).** While you speak, the voice field fills with light that speeds up with your voice, and it now moves on every way you dictate.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
