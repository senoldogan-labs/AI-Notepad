Gistsmith 0.2.0 syncs your meetings between Mac and iPhone, gives both apps a calmer design and writes more accurate notes.

## New
- **Sync between Mac and iPhone.** Turn it on in Settings to see your meetings, notes, tasks, people and saved Ask chats on both devices. Everything is encrypted on your device before it leaves, with a key only your devices hold. Audio and screenshots stay on the device that recorded them. Sync is off until you turn it on.
- **A calmer iPhone app.** One home screen replaces the tab bar. It shows today's meetings, your open tasks and your recent notes. Ask anything from the bar at the bottom, and start recording with the big button next to it.
- **A quieter meeting page.** Your note comes first, under a simple header with the date, length and language. Export, share and the other actions are in one menu. Talk time and the glossary open from Details.
- **Your profile.** Add your job title, company and a short description of what your company does. Notes use them to read product names and jargon correctly and to know whose side you are on.
- **Template gallery.** Browse ready-made note templates by category, including team, product, sales, hiring and learning. Use one as it is or edit a copy to make it your own.
- **Companies.** People are now also grouped by company, so you can see everyone you meet from one organisation together.
- **A guided first meeting.** New users can try Gistsmith on a short sample meeting before their first real one.

## Improvements
- **More accurate notes.** Tasks go to the right person, even when people say "you" to each other in the meeting. Notes also list open questions and show when each decision was made.
- **Better translation.** After the meeting, the full transcript is translated again piece by piece, so nothing is left untranslated and the wording is clearer. Any part that could not be translated can be retried.
- **Read back during a recording.** On iPhone you can scroll up through the live transcript while recording, and it follows along again when you scroll back to the bottom.
- **Tidier settings.** Settings are grouped into cards with short descriptions.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
