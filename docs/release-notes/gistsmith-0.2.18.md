Gistsmith 0.2.18 adds a free plan, so Gistsmith keeps working after your trial, and lets you use the iPhone keyboard without an account.

## New
- **A free plan.** After your trial, Gistsmith keeps working for free: recordings and transcripts written on your device with no limit, 4 AI notes a month for meetings up to 45 minutes, and 5 questions a month in Ask. Your meetings and notes stay on that device. Personal adds speakers and names, live translation, a note for every meeting and sync between Mac and iPhone. On iPhone, the free plan needs iOS 26 or later.
- **The keyboard without an account (iPhone).** Tap "Use the keyboard without an account" to set up the Gistsmith Keyboard right away: typing, swiping, Turkish letters and voice typing on your iPhone work with no sign-in. Sign in later for meeting notes and Ask; your keyboard settings stay as they are.

## Improvements
- **Recording continues past your monthly hours.** When your Personal or Pro hours for the month are used up, new recordings are written on your device instead of stopping. Live translation and live questions come back when your hours renew.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
