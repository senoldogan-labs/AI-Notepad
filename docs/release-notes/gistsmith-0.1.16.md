Gistsmith 0.1.16 puts the names you give speakers everywhere in your notes, and Ask can look at what was on screen.

## New
- **Ask sees your screens.** With "Use screen snapshots in notes" on, Ask also looks at up to three screen snapshots from the moments it reads, and tells you how many it used, like "6 passages and 2 screen snapshots read".

## Improvements
- **Speaker names everywhere.** When you name the people in a meeting, the summary, topics, decisions, tasks and glossary now use those names right away instead of "Speaker 1" and "Speaker 2", and so do exports, shared notes and the notes sent to your connected apps. Rename someone and the note follows.
- **Your tasks, found.** Tasks given to the speaker you named after yourself now show up as yours in Tasks.
- **Fewer, better screen snapshots.** Gistsmith now keeps a snapshot when the shared screen really changes, like a new slide or page, and no longer fills the Screens tab with frames from a playing video.
- **Screens tab in narrow windows.** The Screens tab fits the side panel: the image sits on top and its time and controls in one line below.
- **Settings explain themselves again.** The explanations are back under each setting instead of behind a question mark.
- **Cleaner meeting cards.** The coloured strip at the top of the meeting cards is gone.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
