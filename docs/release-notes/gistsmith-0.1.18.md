Gistsmith 0.1.18 makes Ask a real conversation, keeps your chats organized and translates what people mean.

## New
- **Ask remembers the conversation.** Follow-up questions keep the earlier answers in mind and stay on the meetings you picked with @, so you can dig deeper without repeating yourself.
- **Saved chats you can find again.** Rename a chat, search all your chats, and filter them to the ones about a meeting or the ones answered from the web.
- **Richer answers.** When a question needs it, Ask answers with tables, step-by-step plans or a ready-to-paste prompt. Copy an answer or save it as a Markdown file.
- **Web search in Ask.** Turn it on in Settings › Ask to let Ask look up what your meetings don't cover, with the web pages listed as sources.
- **Translation that keeps the meaning.** Live translation now carries what the speaker means instead of translating word for word: questions stay questions, and promises keep who, what and when.

## Improvements
- **Notes are ready sooner** after a meeting ends.
- **Long and interrupted meetings finish on their own.** If a long meeting or a meeting interrupted by sleep or a restart couldn't finish its note, Gistsmith completes it without you having to do anything.
- **A calmer Ask start page** and a simpler meeting overview.
- **Safer by default.** Links in Ask answers open only web pages; voice typing no longer puts copied passwords back on the clipboard; and diagnostics you send to support leave out keys and personal details.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
