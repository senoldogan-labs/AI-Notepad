Gistsmith 0.2.9 gives the Mac a calmer home page, makes Olive the standard look and lets you see an example note before you sign in.

## New
- **A calmer home on Mac.** Home now shows your next meeting, your recent meetings grouped by day and a place to ask about all your meetings, with a tidier sidebar and search next to the window buttons.
- **See an example before you sign in.** "See an example note" on the sign-in screen opens a sample meeting's note and transcript, and you can listen to it, on Mac and iPhone.

## Improvements
- **Olive is the standard look.** New installs start in Olive on Mac, iPhone and the widgets. A look you already picked stays as it is.
- **Readable Share button in dark mode (iPhone).** The Share button on a meeting page is now easy to read in dark mode.
- **Tidier People page (iPhone).** The People page lays out correctly before anyone has been added.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
