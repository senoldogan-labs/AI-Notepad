Gistsmith 0.1.19 gets room recordings right on the Mac and brings your in-person tasks to My tasks.

## Improvements
- **Better transcripts of meetings recorded in the room.** When you record a meeting around the table on your Mac without turning on "In person", Gistsmith now notices there was no call and separates the speakers just like an in-person recording, with the same careful transcription. Use "Reprocess" on earlier meetings to fix them.
- **Your in-person tasks in My tasks.** In an in-person meeting you are one of the speakers, so Gistsmith now asks which one you were, with each speaker's first sentence to help you recognise yourself. Pick yourself and your tasks move to My tasks. My tasks lists the meetings still waiting for your answer.
- **Crash reports always send.** Large crash reports are trimmed to fit, keeping what's needed to find the problem.
- **A cleaner "Add person" window** that follows your theme and accent colour.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
