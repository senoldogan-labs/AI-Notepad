Gistsmith 0.1.17 speaks your language, writes your follow-up email and stops recording when the call is over.

## New
- **Gistsmith in your language.** The app is now available in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Finnish, as well as English and Turkish. It follows your Mac's language, and you can pick another one in Settings › General.
- **Follow-up email in one click.** Choose "Write follow-up email" in a meeting's ⋯ menu and Gistsmith drafts a short email from the note: the key points, the decisions and the action items with owners and due dates. Edit it, copy it or open it in Mail with the meeting's attendees already filled in.
- **Recording stops when the call does.** When your Zoom, Teams, Meet or FaceTime call ends, Gistsmith shows a 30-second countdown and then finishes the recording, so you don't keep recording an empty room. Choose "Keep recording" to carry on, or turn it off in Settings.
- **Open tasks at the start of the next meeting.** When you record a meeting from your calendar, Gistsmith shows the unfinished tasks from earlier meetings in the same series or with the same people.
- **The right template from the meeting's name.** A meeting called "1:1 with Aino", "Daily stand-up" or "Sprint planning" gets the matching note template automatically. Two new templates, Daily stand-up and Sprint planning, keep per-person updates, blockers, sprint goals and risks in their own sections. You can still pick any template yourself.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
