Gistsmith 0.2.14 makes the Gistsmith Keyboard a complete keyboard on iPhone: ask your meetings where you type, speak in one language and write in another, and let voice typing tidy your words.

## New
- **Ask Gistsmith without leaving your app (iPhone).** Ask a question about your meetings from the Gistsmith Keyboard. The whole answer appears on a card in place of the keys; add it to your message, copy it, or ask again, and undo it with one tap after adding. Needs Full Access, and answers come from your meetings when the AI assistant connection or Meeting notes in the keyboard is on.
- **Translate what you say (iPhone).** Speak one language and the keyboard writes another. Translation happens on the iPhone with Apple's translation languages, which you can download from Settings › Keyboard. Requires iOS 26 or later.
- **Polished voice typing (iPhone).** Choose the Polished style and Apple Intelligence fixes misheard words, grammar and punctuation on the iPhone before your words go in. Requires an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
- **Dictation bar (iPhone).** Prefer to only talk? Switch the keyboard to a slim bar with voice typing, space, delete and return.
- **Say the punctuation (iPhone).** Say "comma", "question mark" or "new line" while voice typing and the mark is written; say "first… second…" and your words become a list.

## Improvements
- **Cleaner voice typing (iPhone).** Ums and stutters are dropped, a dictation in the middle of a sentence starts in lowercase, and you can choose whether it ends with a period or is written all in lowercase.
- **Undo and paste dictation (iPhone).** Take back the last dictation with one tap, even after moving the cursor, and if your words couldn't go in, Paste dictation puts them in.
- **Your keyboard, your way (iPhone).** Pick QWERTY, QWERTZ or AZERTY for each language, get emoji suggestions as you type, choose haptics in three strengths and put the microphone key on either side.
- **See what the keyboard learned (iPhone).** Settings › Keyboard lists the words it learned, lets you delete them or stop it learning, and has a field to try the keyboard.
- **A better space bar (iPhone).** Hold or drag the space bar to move the cursor smoothly, and a quick tap always types a space.
- **A livelier voice field (iPhone).** While you speak, the voice field fills with light that speeds up with your voice, and it keeps moving after you come back from Gistsmith.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
