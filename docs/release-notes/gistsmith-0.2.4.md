Gistsmith 0.2.4 fixes how the app looks when you pick a fixed theme or a different font.

## Improvements
- **Readable in every theme.** Choosing Light, Dark, Sand, Midnight or Graphite yourself no longer leaves some cards and dividers in the wrong colours, and they update the moment you switch.
- **Fonts that fit your language.** The font picker only lists fonts that include every letter the app needs, so words with letters like ş, ğ or â no longer mix two typefaces. If a font you chose earlier is missing letters, Gistsmith uses Inter instead.
- **Clearer text.** Section headings, speaker names and a few buttons have more contrast in light and dark.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
