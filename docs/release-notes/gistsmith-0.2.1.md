Gistsmith 0.2.1 lets you share a note with an end-to-end encrypted link, connect your AI assistant to your meetings, and polishes the iPhone app.

## New
- **Share a note with a link.** In a meeting's ⋯ menu, choose Share with a link. The note is encrypted on your device and the key stays in the link, so only people who have the link can read it; Gistsmith can't. Links expire after 7, 30 or 90 days, and you can remove a link at any time.
- **AI assistant access.** Connect Claude or ChatGPT to your meetings in Settings › Connections. Your assistant can then search and read your notes, but never your audio or transcripts. It's off until you turn it on, and turning it off deletes the copy straight away.

## Improvements
- **A tidier iPhone home.** The meeting language and live translation now sit under the date, so the bottom bar shows just Ask and the record button.
- **Cleaner icons.** Meetings show a calm sound-wave icon instead of a letter, and settings icons no longer have a border.
- **Clearer directions.** Hints that still pointed at the old Record and Meetings tabs now point at the home screen.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
