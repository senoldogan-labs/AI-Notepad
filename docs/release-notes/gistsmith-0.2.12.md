Gistsmith 0.2.12 lets you voice type on iPhone without switching apps, and the Gistsmith Keyboard now wears your theme.

## New
- **Voice type without switching apps (iPhone).** Tap the microphone in the Gistsmith Keyboard and talk: the words appear where you are typing, in any app, and Gistsmith no longer opens in between. Your voice is transcribed on the iPhone, and the microphone is only on while you speak. Requires iOS 26 or later.

## Improvements
- **A keyboard in your colours (iPhone).** The Gistsmith Keyboard follows the palette and accent you chose in Appearance, and voice typing gets a waveform that moves with your voice.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
