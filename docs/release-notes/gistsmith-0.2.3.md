Gistsmith 0.2.3 lets you pick your microphone, ask anything from one place, and brings a warm new look.

## New
- **Choose your microphone.** Pick any connected microphone in Settings › Recording and languages, or switch from the record bar or the menu bar while you record. Plugging in or unplugging a headset no longer stops a recording.
- **One Ask for every question.** Ask about all your meetings, this meeting, a folder, a person or the last 7 or 30 days from the same box, or open it over any page with ⌘J. Tap the microphone to ask by voice. Answers come back in the language you asked in.
- **Ember, a warm new look.** Cream and copper in light mode, warm near-black in dark mode. Prefer the old look? Choose Classic in Settings › Appearance.
- **Spotlight, Siri and Shortcuts.** Find meetings in Spotlight, ask your meetings or hear your open tasks through Siri, and start, pause or stop a recording from Shortcuts.
- **Photos in your notes.** Add a photo of a whiteboard or slide from a file, by dragging it in, or straight from your iPhone camera. It is used in the note when "Use screen snapshots in notes" is on.
- **Turn your mic on mid-meeting.** If you started without your own voice, switch it on at any time; the transcript marks where you joined.
- **Real due dates for tasks.** "Friday" written last week now shows as overdue. Tasks are grouped by Overdue, Today, Next 7 days and Later, and you can add your own.

## Improvements
- **Paused time doesn't count.** Time spent paused no longer counts toward your monthly hours.
- **Insights, full width.** The Insights page is now a dashboard with trends compared to the previous period.
- **Tidier Settings.** Settings are grouped into clear pages, with your plan at the top.
- **Your font everywhere.** Every text in the app now follows the font, size and weight you choose in Appearance.
- **More reliable recording.** A recording that's starting can be cancelled, a stalled microphone reconnects by itself, and if a note can't be finished your original recording is kept so Try again works.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
