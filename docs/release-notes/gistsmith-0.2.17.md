Gistsmith 0.2.17 makes the Gistsmith Keyboard on iPhone learn how you type, understand two languages in one sentence and write Turkish right even without the marks.

## New
- **A keyboard that learns your touch (iPhone).** The keyboard learns on your iPhone where you actually tap each key and moves the keys' touch areas to match, so fewer taps land on the neighbour. It keeps no text, the middle of a key you see always types that key, and you can reset it in Settings › Keyboard.
- **Two languages in one sentence (iPhone).** Speak Turkish and English, or any two of your keyboard languages, in the same sentence and each part is written in its own language. Requires iOS 26 or later and both speech languages on the iPhone; turn it off in Settings › Keyboard.
- **Turkish letters come back (iPhone).** Type without the marks and the keyboard writes the word right: "gorusme" becomes "görüşme", "cok" becomes "çok". Delete right after to keep what you typed.
- **Your names, spelled your way (iPhone).** Voice typing writes the people and terms in your vocabulary exactly as you listed them, such as "Stacey" rather than "Stacy".
- **Emoji shortcodes (iPhone).** Type a colon and a name, such as ":pizza", and pick the emoji from the suggestion bar.
- **More ways to type (iPhone).** In Settings › Keyboard: comma and period keys beside the space bar, quick accents (hold a letter and let go to type its main accent), delete swiped words letter by letter, hide word suggestions, and keep what the keyboard learns separate for each language.

## Improvements
- **Swipe again for the next word (iPhone).** Swipe a word, delete it and swipe again: the keyboard offers the next word, and the one you deleted waits in the suggestion bar.
- **Keys where you expect them (iPhone).** The keys sit where the iPhone's own keyboard has them, in portrait and landscape, and the letters keep their full size in address and email fields.
- **A keyboard that stays (iPhone).** The keyboard no longer uses more memory each time it appears, which could make the iPhone switch back to its own keyboard.
- **Cleaner Japanese and Chinese dictation (iPhone).** No stray spaces next to your words.

Requires iOS 18 or later.
