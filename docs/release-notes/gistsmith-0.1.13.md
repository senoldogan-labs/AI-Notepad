Gistsmith 0.1.13 lets you type with your voice in any Mac app and makes getting started clearer.

## New
- **Voice typing in any app.** Hold Control-Option-D, speak, and let go: your words are typed where your cursor is, in Mail, Slack, your browser or any other app. Tap the shortcut once to dictate hands-free, and tap it again (or press Esc) to finish. Speech is turned into text on your Mac, and voice typing is free with any Gistsmith account. The first time, macOS asks for Accessibility permission so Gistsmith can paste the text for you. You can change the shortcut or turn voice typing off in Settings.

## Improvements
- **A clearer start.** Before your first meeting, the Meetings page shows how to start recording or import an audio or video file, and your next calendar meeting if you have one. Tasks, People and Ask have tidier empty screens too.
- **Account details.** Settings › Account shows where your subscription is managed, and the invite card explains the friend reward more clearly.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
