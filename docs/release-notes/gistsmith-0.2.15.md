Gistsmith 0.2.15 shows which lines of a note came from your own notes, adds the Pro plan on iPhone, and makes the Gistsmith Keyboard quicker.

## New
- **Your notes stand out.** When you write in My notes during a meeting, the finished note shows which summary lines, decisions and open questions came from what you wrote: they have a coloured dot and full-strength text, and the lines added from the recording are a little lighter.
- **Gistsmith Pro on iPhone.** Need more than 10 meeting hours a month? Pro gives you 30, with everything Personal has. You can switch between Personal and Pro at any time.

## Improvements
- **My notes, closer at hand.** On the Mac, My notes stays visible under the live transcript while you record; on iPhone it sits in the control bar next to Highlight.
- **Quicker keys (iPhone).** Every key of the Gistsmith Keyboard reacts at once, including the keys at the edges of the screen.
- **Steadier voice typing (iPhone).** The voice field stays on screen until your words are in, and when Gistsmith doesn't answer, the keyboard writes down what you say itself instead of opening the app (iOS 26 or later).

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
